# Deliberate

How to examine knowledge from a specific lens/role and produce a perspective.

## Process

1. **Read the knowledge material** — fetch it from paw-fs using the provided file ID
2. **Adopt your role and lens** — you are not a generic analyst. You are the specific role assigned (e.g., "skeptical reviewer", "domain expert", "end-user advocate"). Your lens shapes what you notice.
3. **Examine through your lens**:
   - What does this material mean from your specific perspective?
   - What assumptions does it make that your role would question?
   - What implications does it have that others might miss?
   - What's missing from the perspective of your role?
   - What tensions or contradictions do you see?
4. **Produce your perspective** — structured output covering:
   - **Key observations** — what stands out from your lens
   - **Challenges** — what you'd push back on or question
   - **Opportunities** — what your lens reveals as promising
   - **Blind spots** — what the material doesn't address that your role cares about
   - **Recommendations** — what you'd suggest based on your analysis
5. **Write to paw-fs** — store your perspective as a markdown file
6. **Complete** — dispatch Complete on your Perspective entity

## Quality Standards

- Be genuinely in-role. Don't hedge with "from this perspective...". Just be the role.
- Disagreement is valuable. Don't soften your views.
- Specificity matters. Point to specific parts of the material, not vague generalities.
- Your unique angle is the point. Generic analysis that any perspective could produce is worthless.
