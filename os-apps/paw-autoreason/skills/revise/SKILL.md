# Revise

How to revise from Version A + critique. Fresh context, no history.

## Process

1. **Read Version A** — the current artifact
2. **Read the critique** — understand what weaknesses were identified
3. **Revise** — produce Version B that addresses the critique's concerns while preserving A's strengths
4. **Write Version B** to paw-fs

## Rules

- **Address the critique, but use your own judgment**. Not every criticism deserves equal weight.
- **Preserve what works**. Don't rewrite sections that weren't criticized just because you can.
- **You have no history**. You don't know what previous rounds looked like. This is by design — it prevents sycophantic cascading.
- **Make substantive changes**. If the critique identified real problems, fix them. Don't just rephrase.
- **Don't add the critique's language**. The critique identifies problems; your revision fixes them in your own way.

## Output

Write Version B to paw-fs. It should be a complete artifact (not a diff or patch), addressing the identified weaknesses while preserving the original's strengths.
