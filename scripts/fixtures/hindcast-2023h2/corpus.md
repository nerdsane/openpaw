# Frozen corpus — consumer AI assistants and chatbots, as of 2023-06-15 (hindcast vantage)

Nothing in this file is dated after 2023-06-15. This is the world the
hindcast engine is allowed to know.

## Products and players (state of play, June 2023)

- ChatGPT (OpenAI): launched Nov 30, 2022; estimated 100M monthly users by
  Jan 2023 — the fastest-growing consumer app on record at the time.
  GPT-4 shipped March 14, 2023 (Plus subscribers only); ChatGPT Plus is
  $20/mo (Feb 2023). Official iOS app since May 18, 2023 (US first, then
  more countries); NO Android app at vantage. Plugins + browsing rolled
  out to Plus users in beta (May 2023). Altman said in April 2023 that
  OpenAI is "not currently training GPT-5"; he testified to the US Senate
  May 16, 2023 and toured world capitals through May-June.
- Microsoft: Bing Chat (Feb 7, 2023), confirmed GPT-4-powered, with ads
  appearing in chat answers since March 2023; Windows Copilot announced
  at Build (May 23, 2023).
- Google: Bard launched March 21, 2023 (US/UK); at I/O (May 10, 2023)
  Bard moved to PaLM 2, dropped the waitlist, and expanded to 180
  countries. Google announced "Gemini" — a next-generation multimodal
  model — as in training, after merging Brain and DeepMind (April 2023).
- Anthropic: Claude available via API, Slack, and Poe since March 2023;
  100K-token context (May 2023); raised a $450M Series C (May 2023).
  NO consumer-facing Claude website at vantage.
- Meta: LLaMA weights released to researchers Feb 24, 2023 (and promptly
  leaked); no consumer assistant shipped at vantage.
- Character.AI: raised $150M at ~$1B (March 2023, a16z); mobile apps
  launched May 2023; engagement among young users is exceptional.
- Inflection AI: Pi, a "personal AI", launched May 2, 2023.
- Snapchat My AI free to all users (April 2023); Quora's Poe (Feb 2023)
  aggregates multiple models.
- Elon Musk: incorporated X.AI Corp (filing reported April 2023) and
  publicly floated a "TruthGPT" rival; no product at vantage.
- Apple: WWDC (June 5-9, 2023) introduced Vision Pro and minor Siri
  changes — no chatbot, no LLM product announced.

## Structural facts already determined at vantage

- The EU Parliament adopted its AI Act negotiating position on June 14,
  2023; trilogue with Council/Commission lies ahead — final agreement
  timing is open. Italy's regulator briefly blocked ChatGPT (April 2023).
- No US federal AI statute exists; Senate hearings are ongoing.
- ChatGPT's mobile presence is iOS-only; voice input/output is not a
  ChatGPT feature at vantage.
- Ads exist in Bing Chat, but ChatGPT, Bard, and Claude carry none.
